Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shagunguna ("Shagunguna", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you access or use our mobile application, website and related marketplace services ("Platform").

Shagunguna is operated by Usama Ibrahim, an individual operator based in Nigeria.

Your privacy matters: We collect and use information to operate our classified marketplace, maintain user accounts, display advertisements, facilitate communication between users, improve our services and protect the Platform against fraud and misuse. We do not sell your personal information as a standalone commercial product.

1. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Shagunguna and which features you choose to access.

A. Account and registration information

When you create or manage an account, we may collect:

  • Your name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number, where provided or required.
  • Username and account identifiers.
  • Profile photographs and other profile information.
  • Authentication and account security information.

B. Listing and advertisement information

When you publish an advertisement, we may collect:

  • Product or service titles and descriptions.
  • Product photographs and uploaded images.
  • Prices and product conditions.
  • Selected categories and listing attributes.
  • Location or delivery information you choose to provide.
  • Seller or business details displayed in your advertisements.

C. Communication information

When you contact other users or communicate with our support team through features provided by Shagunguna, we may process relevant messages, reports, attachments and communication records necessary to operate the service, investigate complaints or protect users.

If you communicate directly through an external service such as a telephone call, SMS or WhatsApp, that communication may be processed independently by the relevant service provider.

D. Device and technical information

We may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Device type and operating system.
  • App version and browser information.
  • IP address and general network information.
  • Device identifiers or installation identifiers, where available.
  • Crash reports, diagnostics and performance information.
  • Usage information and interactions with Platform features.

E. Location information

We may process location information when you choose to provide a location for an advertisement or enable location-related features.

Where the App requests device location permission, location information will be accessed according to the permission granted and the feature being used.

You can manage or revoke location permissions through your device settings. Some location-based features may not work properly if permission is disabled.

F. Payment and subscription information

If Shagunguna introduces paid features, subscriptions, promotions or integrated payment services, we may process information necessary to manage those services, such as transaction references, purchase status, subscription status and payment-related records.

Payment card details and other sensitive financial credentials should be handled by the relevant payment provider where an external payment service is used. We do not claim to store payment card information unless a specific payment feature expressly requires and securely supports it.

2. How we use your information

We may use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Creating, maintaining and authenticating user accounts.
  • Publishing and managing classified advertisements.
  • Displaying listings and connecting buyers with sellers.
  • Providing communication and notification features.
  • Responding to customer support enquiries.
  • Investigating reports, suspicious listings and fraudulent activities.
  • Protecting account security and preventing unauthorized access.
  • Improving application performance, usability and reliability.
  • Managing subscriptions, promotions and paid services where available.
  • Complying with applicable laws and lawful requests from authorities.
  • Enforcing our Terms of Use and marketplace policies.

3. Public information and marketplace visibility

Shagunguna is a classified marketplace. Certain information is intended to be visible to other users or the public when you publish an advertisement or complete your profile.

Depending on your settings and the features you use, publicly visible information may include:

  • Your display name or seller name.
  • Profile photograph.
  • Product or service photographs.
  • Advertisement descriptions and prices.
  • Business or seller information.
  • Location information you choose to include.
  • Contact information you choose to display.

Please do not publish private or sensitive information in public advertisements.

Information that you make public may be copied, saved, shared or accessed by people outside Shagunguna. Removing a listing does not necessarily remove copies previously saved or shared by other users.

4. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable data-protection laws require a legal basis for processing personal information, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contractual necessity: Processing required to create accounts and provide marketplace services you request.
  • Legitimate interests: Processing necessary to secure, maintain and improve the Platform, prevent fraud and protect users, provided those interests do not override your rights.
  • Consent: Processing based on your permission, such as optional location access or certain marketing communications.
  • Legal obligations: Processing necessary to comply with applicable laws, regulations and lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone commercial product. However, we may share relevant information in the following circumstances:

A. With other users

Information included in your public profile, advertisements or communications may be shared with other Shagunguna users as necessary for marketplace activities.

These providers may process information on our behalf under applicable contractual, security and confidentiality obligations.

We may disclose information when required or permitted by applicable law, court orders, lawful government requests or other legitimate legal processes.

Shagunguna may depend on or integrate with third-party services. Their processing of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Secure communication through encrypted connections where supported.
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms.
  • Server and database security measures.

You may request the deletion of your Shagunguna account and associated personal information through the account-management or account-deletion options provided in the App, where available.

If you cannot access an account-deletion option, you may contact our support team using the contact details below.
