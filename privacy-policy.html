In brief: Arayuwa does not require you to create an account. Subscription transactions are processed through Apple or Google and RevenueCat. If you choose an optional AI feature or send a prompt to an outside AI app, the information included in that prompt may be processed by that service.

1. Information you enter in Arayuwa

Depending on the features you use, you may enter information such as a name, date of birth, a question, chart selections or figures, relationship details, wish text, and app preferences. These details are used to generate the calculations, chart interpretations, saved results and other features you request.

On-device storage: Core calculations and saved information are designed to be processed and stored locally on your device. Arayuwa does not require account registration and, for those local features, does not send the information to an Arayuwa user-account database. Information stored on your device may remain there until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Device backup settings may affect whether local app data is included in a device backup.

2. Subscriptions and purchase information

Arayuwa offers optional Premium subscriptions. Purchases are completed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number or banking credentials.

Arayuwa uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status, validate purchases, and support restore-purchase functionality. When the RevenueCat SDK is used, RevenueCat may receive an automatically generated anonymous App User ID, purchase and subscription history, transaction or receipt information, purchase tokens, device/operating-system technical information, and related information needed to operate the subscription service. RevenueCat processes this information under its own privacy policy: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Apple and Google also process purchase information under their respective privacy policies and payment terms. Subscription management, billing, cancellations and refunds are handled through the store from which you purchased.

3. Optional AI features and external AI apps

Some versions or features of Arayuwa may let you request an AI-assisted interpretation, generate an AI prompt, or open/share a prompt with an AI service. When you choose to use such a feature, the question, chart figures, and other details included in the request or prompt may be sent to the relevant Arayuwa service or third-party AI provider so it can generate a response. If you copy a prompt into another AI app yourself, that provider receives the information you choose to paste.

These services are operated by their respective providers and are subject to their own privacy terms and retention practices. Do not include information in an AI prompt that you do not want the selected provider to process. If a version of Arayuwa does not offer an AI feature, this section does not describe a data transfer taking place in that version.

4. How information is used

Information is used to provide the feature you request, save your selected results on your device, maintain and restore Premium access, respond to support requests, protect the App and its services, and comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the App, or our legal rights.
